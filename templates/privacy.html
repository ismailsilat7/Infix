Infix Privacy Policy

Welcome to Infix. Your privacy is a top priority for us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our website. By using Infix, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information We Collect

Personal Data: When you register or log in, we collect personal information such as your name, email address, and, if you sign in via Google, your Google profile information.

Course Data: We track your selected paths (O Level / A Level), enrolled courses, bookmarked topics, and progress.

Usage Data: We collect information on how you interact with our site, including visited pages, your study preferences, and session activity.

Contact Information: If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information such as your name, email address, phone number, and the content of your message.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Operate and Maintain: Provide, operate, and maintain our website.

Personalize: Improve and personalize your experience on our site.

Analyze: Understand how you use our website to enhance functionality.

Communicate: Send updates, respond to queries, and provide support.

Develop: Create new features and improve our offerings.

Protect: Find and prevent fraud.

Cookies

Infix uses cookies to manage user sessions and enhance website performance. Cookies help us remember your preferences and track usage. You can manage cookie settings through your browser, but disabling cookies may affect your experience.

Data Transfers

By using Infix, you consent to the transfer of your data to servers located in Pakistan.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services (e.g., Google OAuth) that may set cookies or collect data. We do not control these services and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Your Rights

Under applicable laws, you have the right to:

Access: Request copies of your personal data.

Rectification: Correct or complete inaccurate information.

Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.

Restriction: Restrict the processing of your data under certain conditions.

Objection: Object to processing of your data under specific circumstances.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at connect.infix@gmail.com

International Users

As Infix is accessible worldwide, your data may be transferred and processed in countries outside your home country. We adhere to basic privacy principles to protect your information.

Children's Information

Infix does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided such information, please contact us to remove it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page, and we will notify you of significant updates. The revised policy will take effect immediately upon posting.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at connect.infix@gmail.com
